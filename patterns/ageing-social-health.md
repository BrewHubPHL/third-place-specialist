# Ageing Social Health via Third Places

**Impact:** HIGH  
**Tags:** ageing, weak-ties, social-health, mobility, accessibility, age-friendly

## When to use

Age-friendly city planning, seniors housing, health policy.

## How

1. Map accessible third places within walking/transit radius (clubs, libraries, MPC commons, mixed centres).
2. Classify ties: strong (family/club), weak (acquaintance), absent (isolation).
3. Remove car dependence as barrier—driving cessation often ends participation.

## Trade-offs

Suburban dispersion makes weak-tie maintenance costly without mobility support.
