# Digital Layer / AR Extension

**Impact:** MEDIUM  
**Tags:** digital-layers, ar, side, code-is-law, virtual-third-place

## When to use

Smart city, heritage trails, game-layer placemaking (Pokémon Go precedent).

## How

1. Apply Oldenburg criteria to mediated layers—does conversation remain primary?
2. Use layering to annotate physical third places without replacing face-to-face gathering.
3. Watch Code-is-law governance—platform rules can exclude more than physical design.

## Trade-offs

Virtual participation may deepen weak ties or isolate; SIDE effects alter social norms.
