# Planning Community vs Enabling Third Places

**Impact:** HIGH  
**Tags:** master-planning, new-urbanism, garden-city, exclusion, community-rhetoric

## When to use

Master-planned neighbourhoods, new towns, eco-villages, mixed-use developments.

## How

1. Map exclusion boundaries (price, gates, car dependence, participation rules).
2. Audit Calthorpe/New Urbanist checklist separately from Oldenburg checklist.
3. Ask: can users modify space without permission? Do weak ties form without scheduled events?

## Trade-offs

Formal public space satisfies planners but may lack conversation primacy.
