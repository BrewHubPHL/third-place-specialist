# Community Garden Third-Place Design

**Impact:** MEDIUM  
**Tags:** community-gardens, social-capital, bonding-bridging, weak-ties

## When to use

Designing or evaluating community gardens as social infrastructure.

## How

1. Prefer open, low-fence, walkable sites over locked club models.
2. Enable over-the-fence bridging; tolerate low-profile "messiness."
3. Anchor with characters/regulars but distribute governance to avoid organiser burnout.
4. Host gardens at existing third places (libraries) to extend neutral ground.

## Trade-offs

Openness increases theft/vandalism risk; exclusivity eases operations but kills bridging.
