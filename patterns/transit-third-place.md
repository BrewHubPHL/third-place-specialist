# Transit Third Place of Mobility

**Impact:** MEDIUM  
**Tags:** transit, mobility-third-place, public-transport, station-design, commute

## When to use

Station precincts, ferry terminals, on-board social design.

## How

1. Treat journey as social time—not only origin-destination efficiency.
2. Design waiting zones for conversation, gift/exchange rituals, and digital layer access.
3. Connect precinct to walkable neighbourhood third places at each end.

## Trade-offs

Commuter throughput pressure conflicts with linger-friendly design.
