# Feminist Third-Place Audit

**Impact:** HIGH  
**Tags:** feminist-geography, emplacement, intersectionality, safety, inclusion

## When to use

Leisure spaces, night economies, parks, transport hubs marketed as "inclusive."

## How

1. Examine who feels safe, visible, and entitled to linger (gender, race, class, ability).
2. Test "neutral ground" claim against surveillance, gaze, and austerity-driven closure of public amenities.
3. Design for emplacement—body-scale transparency, escape routes, diverse programming.

## Trade-offs

Safety interventions can over-surveil and exclude; balance transparency with autonomy.
