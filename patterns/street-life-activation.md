# Street Life Zone Activation (Pavement Dining)

**Impact:** MEDIUM  
**Tags:** street-life, pavement-dining, three-zones, gehl, main-street

## When to use

Main street revitalisation, café strip planning, Geelong-style audits.

## How

1. Map Three Zones: Zone 1 (outdoor dining), Zone 2 (sidewalk activity), Zone 3 (street).
2. Favour soft edges, personalisation, greenery over hard façades.
3. Count activities, group sizes, and time-of-day patterns (Bell Park methodology).

## Trade-offs

Outdoor dining drives Zone 1 but requires weather, policy, and rent structures.
