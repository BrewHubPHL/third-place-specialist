# Evidence-Based Third-Place Design

**Impact:** MEDIUM  
**Tags:** place-making, green-planning, lively-planning, cilliers, design-synthesis

## When to use

Redesigning public spaces in Belgium/South Africa case contexts; transferable design checklist.

## How

1. Apply place-making (aesthetics, sense of place), green planning, and lively planning lenses.
2. Evaluate re-coded design elements per case study (Jardin de la Maison, Louvain-La-Neuve, etc.).
3. Prioritize social activity quality over physical equipment; context-driven cultural design over generic visibility.

## Trade-offs

Visibility (CPTED) ranked lower than social programming in Cilliers' synthesis.
