# Eyes on the Street Safety Assessment

**Impact:** MEDIUM  
**Tags:** jacobs, cpted, crime, safety, natural-surveillance

## When to use

Neighbourhood safety planning, third-place clustering in retail strips.

## How

1. Inventory third places and natural surveillance along routes.
2. Compare perceived vs recorded crime; note modifying factors (lighting, land use, density, socio-economics).
3. Do not assume more third places = less crime—Chirn Park/Mermaid Waters counterexamples.

## Trade-offs

Activity and "eyes" help perception; crime correlates with broader urban form and economy.
