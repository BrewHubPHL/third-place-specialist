# Cheatsheet — Rethinking Third Places

## Is it a third place? (Oldenburg 8-test)

| # | Criterion | Pass if… | Fail signal |
|---|-----------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | Neutral ground | Neither home nor work; voluntary meeting | Members-only, gated, purchase-required |
| 2 | Leveller | Status differences fade | Exclusive pricing, dress codes, VIP zones |
| 3 | Easy access | Walkable, accommodates linger + activity | Car-only, hostile to non-consumers |
| 4 | Regulars | Known characters anchor social life | Pure transient traffic |
| 5 | Low profile | Informal, unpretentious | Corporate polish, performance venue |
| 6 | Playful | Humour, games, light mood | Silent transit, punitive rules |
| 7 | Home away from home | Emotional comfort, return habit | Nowhere to sit, no ownership feeling |
| 8 | Conversation primary | Talk is the main activity | Shopping/through-traffic dominates |

**Rule**: Score ≥6 with conversation + neutral ground mandatory before calling it a third place.

---

## Planning trap vs third-place enabler

| Situation | Do | Because |
|-----------|-----|---------|
| New Urbanist "town centre" planned | Run Oldenburg audit separately from form checklist | Mixed-use form ≠ conversation |
| Master plan claims "community" | Map exclusion (price, gates, car need) | Community rhetoric hides who is out |
| CPTED demands visibility | Pair with social programming (Cilliers) | Eyes alone ≠ safety (Holden) |
| Garden locked for vandalism | Accept trade-off or hybrid open hours | Clubs build bonding not bridging |
| Transit station redesign | Design wait time as social time (O'Hare) | Mobility-only design kills weak ties |

## Social capital quick pick

| Need | Prefer | Avoid |
|------|--------|-------|
| Integrate newcomers | Open garden, café regulars, transit hub | Locked club, interest-only membership |
| Support ageing in place | Walkable club/library/MPC within 10 min | Car-dependent suburban centre |
| Child social health | Free playful public realm (South Bank model) | Programmed-only paid activities |
| Neighbourhood safety perception | Mixed use + active frontages + lighting | Assuming retail strip = safe |
| Heritage/community memory | DIY archive as third place (Ch 7) | Museum without participant ownership |

## Design decision tree — community garden

```
Open to public without key?
├─ NO → Club model (bonding only) — OK for specialist interest
└─ YES → Walkable from members' homes?
    ├─ NO → Weak third-place localness
    └─ YES → Conversation at beds/fence?
        ├─ NO → Add commons tasks, events, blackboard
        └─ YES → 8-characteristic audit → likely authentic third place
```

## Street activation (Ch 11)

| Physical trait | Effect on activity |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Soft edge + outdoor dining | Highest Zone 1 activation |
| Personalised shopfront | Extends third-place identity to street |
| Greenery/landscaping | Supports linger, not sufficient alone |
| Hard edge façade | Suppresses sidewalk social life |

**Default**: Prioritise pavement dining permissions and soft edges before beautification alone.

---

## Digital layer rule

**When adding AR/app layer to a physical place**: Use layering to *support* existing gathering—never replace face-to-face conversation as primary activity. Check platform governance (who can be excluded by code).
