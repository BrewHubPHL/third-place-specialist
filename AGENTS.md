## Usage

1. Read `SKILL.md` for routing, principles, and progressive disclosure map.
2. Load **one** `patterns/*.md` file matching the task — avoid reading the whole tree.
3. Check `anti-patterns.md` before endorsing "community" or "third place" claims.
4. For worked scenarios, read `examples/third-place-planning.md`.
5. For chapter evidence, start at [references/book-summaries/rtp-index.md](references/book-summaries/rtp-index.md) and load **one** chapter file.

## When to apply

- Master plans, MPCs, or "community hub" briefs claiming social cohesion
- Community garden, library, or café design reviews
- Age-friendly, child-friendly, or transit precinct planning
- CPTED / safety claims tied to retail strips or third-place clustering
- Smart city, AR, or heritage layers on physical places
- Book chapter lookup from *Rethinking Third Places*

## Operating principles

Domain-specific third-place philosophy lives in [PRINCIPLES.md](PRINCIPLES.md). Apply on top of the patterns in this repo:

1. Conversation is the test, not the label
2. Planning enables or destroys; it rarely installs community
3. Weak ties are infrastructure
4. No place is neutral
5. Layer, don't replace
